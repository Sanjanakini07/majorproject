Connect a phone

  1. Connect the phone to the same Wi-Fi or hotspot as this laptop.
  2. Scan this code with the phone camera.
  3. The phone warns that the connection is not private. That is expected: this laptop made its own certificate. Tap Advanced, then Proceed (on iPhone: Show details → visit this website).

If the phone can't open it, allow Python through the Windows firewall on private networks (Windows asks the first time the app starts).
